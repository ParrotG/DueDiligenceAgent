# Iterative research

- Restate the task as something a source could confirm or contradict. Identify the most useful document class and the entity and date that must match.
- Obtain the original when possible. Record the relevant text or data, document location, publisher, publication date, effective date and direct URL. Note what the source cannot establish.
- Test the provisional answer against a plausible alternative, a later update and an independent source where material. Identify reposts, common data origins, and interests that affect attribution or interpretation.
- Continue only when a specific new check is likely to change the answer or resolve a material gap. At the stopping point, separate established facts, reasonable inferences, conflicting accounts and unresolved items.

Reference: [Foundry Deep Research](https://github.com/foundry-works/foundry-research/blob/main/skills/deep-research/SKILL.md).
