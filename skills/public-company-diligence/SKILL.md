# Public-source company diligence

1. Build an identity map. Record legal names, registration numbers, ticker and exchange, old names, operating brands, parent entities and the effective dates of changes. Tie each alias to the legal entity or product it actually describes.
2. Ask for the decision the research should inform if the answer changes scope. Use the entity, sector, jurisdictions, date window, material issues and intended audience to decide which checks merit attention.
3. Screen ownership and governance, business model and major dependencies, material financial or operational changes, disputes and regulatory issues, and sector-specific risks. Select the relevant areas and explain their priority.
4. For each selected area, identify a testable question, an independently known primary source or official source family, a completion criterion, and the disclosure gap if the information is private. Give priority to risks that could change the user's decision.

Reference: [Anthropic due diligence checklist](https://github.com/anthropics/financial-services/blob/main/plugins/vertical-plugins/private-equity/skills/dd-checklist/SKILL.md). This project uses its scoping and workstream ideas for public records.
